# BIST Algorithmic Trading Challenge
## Python Backtesting Assignment

### Objective
Design and implement one algorithmic trading strategy in Python for the following six BIST stocks: AKBNK, ASELS, TUPRS, TCELL, FROTO, and EREGL.

* **Backtest period:** 1 January 2025 - 1 October 2026
* **Initial capital:** 100,000 TL per stock
* The same core strategy must be applied to all six stocks.
* **Goal:** outperform the benchmark result for every stock individually.

### Benchmark Results

| Stock | Initial Capital | Benchmark Net Profit | Benchmark Final Capital |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AKBNK** | 100,000 TL | +84,000 TL | 184,000 TL |
| **ASELS** | 100,000 TL | +239,000 TL | 339,000 TL |
| **TUPRS** | 100,000 TL | +72,000 TL | 172,000 TL |
| **TCELL** | 100,000 TL | -18,000 TL | 82,000 TL |
| **FROTO** | 100,000 TL | +3,000 TL | 103,000 TL |
| **EREGL** | 100,000 TL | +39,000 TL | 139,000 TL |

**Important:** overall portfolio profit is not used for passing the challenge. Each stock must beat its own benchmark individually.

**Example:**
Suppose your strategy produces:
* AKBNK: +90,000 TL $\rightarrow$ PASS, because the benchmark is +84,000 TL
* ASELS: +250,000 TL $\rightarrow$ PASS, because the benchmark is +239,000 TL
* TUPRS: +60,000 TL $\rightarrow$ FAIL, because the benchmark is +72,000 TL

Even if your total portfolio profit is higher than the benchmark total, the challenge is not passed because TUPRS did not beat its own benchmark. You must beat the benchmark for all six stocks individually.

### Student Task
Create one algorithmic trading system and apply it to all six stocks. You may either use one of the four strategies covered in class or design and implement your own trading system.

* You may use indicators such as moving averages, RSI, Bollinger Bands, momentum, volume, ATR, support/resistance, breakouts, mean reversion, trailing stops, or machine-learning features.
* Trade decisions must be generated by Python rules, not by manually selected dates.
* At least 3 completed trades must be generated for every stock.
* You must design and implement a trading strategy and clearly present the complete results. Your submission must include your source code, all buy and sell times, the exact buy and sell prices for each trade, the profit or loss of each completed trade, and a chart showing the trades and their resulting profits/losses.

### Required Python Workflow
* Load historical price data.
* Calculate indicators or features.
* Generate BUY and SELL signals.
* Apply position sizing and risk management.
* Run the backtest.
* Calculate performance metrics.
* Compare each stock with the benchmark.

You are allowed to modify, extend, or improve these strategies before applying them to the six stocks.

### No Look-Ahead Rule
The strategy must use only information available at the time of the trade. Future prices, future returns, or manually selected best entry/exit dates are not allowed.

### Risk Management Requirement
The strategy must include at least one risk-management mechanism, such as a stop-loss, ATR stop, trailing stop, Chandelier Exit, maximum holding period, or risk-based position sizing.

### Required Metrics

* Final Capital
* Total Trades
* Winning Trades
* Losing Trades
* Win Rate

* Profit Factor
* Average Trade

### Final Analysis Question
Explain why the same strategy performed differently across the six stocks. 
Discuss factors such as trending vs. sideways markets, volatility, false breakouts, momentum strength, reversal behavior, volume characteristics, and stop-loss frequency.
